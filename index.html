Published Research

Peer-reviewed work from my M.Sc. at AASTMT on computer vision and weakly supervised learning.

Algorithms (MDPI) 2026, 19(10), 811 Peer-reviewed Open Access IF 2.6 · CiteScore 5.4

HRM-Crime: A Hierarchical Relationship Model for Weakly Supervised Crime Anomaly Detection in Surveillance Video

Eslam Medhat Fathy Habib, Ayman Helmy, Mohamed Mostafa Fouad · Published 22 September 2026

Detecting crime in surveillance footage when the only label says "something happened somewhere in this video", with no timestamps or bounding boxes. Instead of scaling up the backbone, HRM-Crime keeps the score model compact, pairing windowed self-attention for local temporal patterns with dilated 1-D convolutions for long-range context. Three copies are trained on deliberately different views of each video (appearance, motion, and both) and fused by rank normalisation.
